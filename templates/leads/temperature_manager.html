How Automated Temperature Updates Work:
  • Initial State: New leads / Meta inquiries start at Hot.
  • Positive Match: Remarks matching positive keywords increase temperature 1 step (up to maximum Hot).
  • Negative Match: Remarks matching negative keywords decrease temperature 1 step: Hot → Warm → Cold → Freeze.
  • Other Remarks: Any comment/remark not in this stack leaves the temperature unchanged as-is.
  • Stage Cancelled: Sets admission status to Lost and temperature directly to Freeze.
